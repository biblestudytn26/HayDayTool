Mussel Linguine is a product unlocked at experience level 73.

Like all products, it is stored in the barn.

Making Mussel Linguine

Mussel linguine is made in the Pasta Kitchen.

To make mussel linguine, players need the following ingredients, in the specified quantities:

x3 x4 x3 x1

It takes 1 hour and 30 minutes to make a plate of mussel linguine, and 1 hour and 16 minutes in a fully mastered kitchen. Each plate gives players 69 experience points.

Selling Mussel Linguine

  • Mussel linguine can be bought or sold for a maximum price of 5,832 Coin for 10 plates.
  • Buying the ingredients required to make veggie lasagna costs 560 Coin coins.

Did you know?

  • Linguine is a popular type of pasta. It is similar to spaghetti but has a unique elliptical, flat-oval cross section rather than a round one. Because of its shape and thickness, linguine is great at catching oil-based, light, or slightly creamy sauces.
